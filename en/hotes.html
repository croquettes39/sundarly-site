What you don't see right away

  • VAT on the commission : the rate shown is not the one you pay. 15,5 % becomes 18,6 %.
  • Imposed discounts : on Booking.com, the Genius programme requires at least 10 % off, and the commission still applies.
  • Commission increases : on Booking.com, the commission now reaches 19 %, and Preferred Partner status adds more points for a better ranking.
  • Commission on cleaning : Airbnb also takes its commission on your cleaning fees, although that money pays for the cleaning work. Here, 77 DH gone out of 414 DH.
  • On Sundarly : 10 % on the nightly price only, VAT included. The cleaning fee is entirely yours.

Owners' questions

Answers to the questions we are asked most.

Can I keep my listing on other platforms?

Yes. Sundarly is added to your other channels. Calendar sync (iCal) automatically blocks dates already booked elsewhere.

How much does Sundarly cost?

Signing up and listing are free. Sundarly takes 10 % of the nightly price of each confirmed booking, never of the cleaning fee, and 0 % on your first 3 bookings if you are among the first 100 hosts to sign up.

When do I get paid?

The guest pays online when booking. You receive 90 % of the amount after they arrive, directly into your bank account.

I don't have time to create the listing, can you do it?

Yes. Send us your photos and a few details; our team prepares the listing and submits it to you before it goes online.

Can I invite my regular clients to book on Sundarly?

Yes, your own contacts (loyal guests, social media followers, visitors to your website) are welcome. However, respect the terms of other platforms: do not solicit there the guests who contacted you through them.
