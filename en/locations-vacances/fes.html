Staying in Fez

Fes el-Bali, its UNESCO-listed medieval medina, is one of the largest car-free urban areas in the world. Tanneries, madrasas, leather and copper craftsmen: staying in a riad in Fez means living to the rhythm of the oldest of Morocco's imperial cities.
