Staying in Agadir

With its long sandy beach and sunshine almost all year round, Agadir is the great seaside resort of southern Morocco. It is ideal for families and sunny winter breaks, with a lively seafront promenade, a marina and many excursions to Paradise Valley or Souss-Massa National Park.
