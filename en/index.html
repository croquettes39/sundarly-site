Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

When will Sundarly be available?

Right now: bookings are open, you can book straight away. And for hosts, 0 % commission on the first three bookings.

Are there any fees for guests?

No. The price shown by the host (nights, cleaning if any, and tourist tax) is the price you pay. Sundarly charges guests no service fee.

How does Sundarly make money?

Through a 10 % commission on the nightly price of each confirmed booking, paid by the owner. Nothing on cleaning fees, nothing for guests.

In which countries?

Sundarly is for vacation rentals in Europe, the Americas, Morocco and Tunisia. The first destinations will open as owners sign up.
