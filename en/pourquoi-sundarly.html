What you don't see right away

  • VAT on the commission : the rate shown is not the one you pay. 15,5 % becomes 18,6 %.
  • Imposed discounts : on Booking.com, the Genius programme requires at least 10 % off, and the commission still applies.
  • Commission increases : on Booking.com, the commission now reaches 19 %, and Preferred Partner status adds more points for a better ranking.
  • Commission on cleaning : Airbnb also takes its commission on your cleaning fees, although that money pays for the cleaning work. Here, 77 DH gone out of 414 DH.
  • On Sundarly : 10 % on the nightly price only, VAT included. The cleaning fee is entirely yours.

On 50 bookings like this one, you keep 11 487 DH more than with Airbnb, and at least 11 925 DH more than with Booking.com.

What protects the owner

  • The guest's name and ID number appear in the agreement
  • Commitment to respect the property and leave it clean
  • Guest's liability for breakage or theft
  • Security deposit if you wish
  • The owner or the property manager can make the guest leave in case of noise or broken rules
  • Legal action possible, under the law of the country where the property is located

What protects the guest

  • The host's name and the exact address of the property in black and white
  • Rules known before paying: times, pets, smoking, parties, deposit
  • The cancellation policy chosen by the host, written into the agreement

  • A host whose identity is verified and a listing checked by our team
  • Written proof in case of disagreement

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we are asked most.

How much does Sundarly cost?

Signing up and listing are free. Sundarly takes 10 % of the nightly price of each confirmed booking, never of the cleaning fee, and 0 % on your first 3 bookings if you are among the first 100 hosts to sign up. Guests pay no service fee.

Can I keep my listing on Airbnb or Booking?

Yes. Sundarly is added to your other channels. Calendar sync (iCal) automatically blocks dates already booked elsewhere.

What is the stay agreement for?

It sets out in writing everyone's rights and duties: guest's identity, property address, respect for the property, cleanliness, liability for breakage or theft, house rules. The guest signs it within 5 minutes of paying, otherwise the booking is cancelled. The owner, the guest and Sundarly each receive a copy.

Why verify everyone's identity?

Because trust has to go both ways. The owner knows who they are hosting, the guest knows whose home they are sleeping in. Without a confirmed identity, it is not possible to rent or to book.

When do I get paid?

The guest pays online when booking. You receive your share (90 % of nights, 100 % of cleaning) after they arrive, directly into your bank account.

When can I book?

Right now: you can book straight away. And for hosts, 0 % commission on the first three bookings.
