About us

A local team, a licensed partner operator

Marrakech Sunrise Balloon organises sunrise balloon flights for travellers staying in Marrakech. We handle your booking, your pickup and your questions from start to finish.

Flights are operated by our partner balloon operator, with qualified pilots and a ground crew that follows the balloon to the landing site.

  • Direct bookingYou talk to us directly on WhatsApp, with no middleman.
  • Safety firstThe pilot decides on every flight according to the weather.
  • Everything includedTransfers, welcome drink, breakfast and certificate.
